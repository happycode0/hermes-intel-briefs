Executive Summary. The current trend cycle is dominated by three interlocking themes: (1) the agent-skills methodology movement (obra/superpowers crossing 285k★, now the de-facto "operating system" for coding agents), (2) a surge of privacy-first, self-hosted AI workspaces — most notably Odysseus, the PewDiePie-built workspace that gained ~77k★ in a month, and (3) a hardening of agentic AI security, led by Cisco's open-source DefenseClaw for OpenClaw governance and the continued rise of crawler-defence tools like Anubis. Cloud-security posture tooling (Prowler's "Agentic Cloud Defender" push, Falco runtime detection) and mature self-hosting staples (Immich, Uptime Kuma) round out a security-conscious, local-first week.

🤖 AI & LLM Architectures

llmfit
AlexsJones/llmfit
⭐ 36.1k

Overview / Mechanics

Rust CLI + TUI that inspects CPU, RAM, GPU/VRAM and accelerator config, then scores 497+ models across 133 providers on fit, speed, quality and context. Exposes a REST API (/api/v1/system, /api/v1/models) and a Docker image. Community benchmarking shares real tok/s back to the project.

Practical Utility

Eliminates guesswork in local-LLM deployment — right-sizes models to your hardware, supports Ollama, llama.cpp, MLX, LM Studio, MoE archs and dynamic quantization.

Target Audience

  • Homelab & edge-AI builders
  • MLOps / platform engineers
  • Local-model enthusiasts
OSINT: Coverage across ajeetraina.com, coddykit.com and llms.org. Described as "one of the fastest-growing developer tools in the local AI ecosystem" with 21k+★ at last measure; now at 36k★ — strong steady upward velocity.

TecharoHQ/anubis
⭐ 22.4k

Overview / Mechanics

Shields small sites from aggressive AI crawlers that ignore robots.txt; an explicit "nuclear" option when Cloudflare isn't viable.

Target Audience
