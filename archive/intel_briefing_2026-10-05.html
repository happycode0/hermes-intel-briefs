p-e-w/heretic

★ 33,242
Python · AGPL-3.0 · #1 Repo of the Day · updated 2026-10-05
Overview / Mechanics

Heretic removes "safety alignment" (censorship) from transformer-based language models without expensive post-training. It packages directional ablation ("abliteration", Arditi et al. NeurIPS 2024) into a fully-automatic one-command pipeline, using a TPE-based optimizer (Optuna) to co-minimize refusal rate and KL divergence from the original model — preserving intelligence while suppressing refusals. Practical Utility

Enables deep-research and red-team users to decensor dense, MoE and some hybrid models (Qwen3.5) in ~20-45 minutes on a single RTX 3090, with bitsandbytes 4-bit quantization to cut VRAM. AGPL-3.0 with a Codeberg mirror. Hotly contested ethically; benchmarked by third parties on MMLU/GSM8K.

  • Local-LLM and uncensored-model enthusiasts, AI safety researchers, red-team operators and abliteration benchmarkers.
