Analyst’s Note (butler’s restraint engaged):

The defining theme this week is containment and trust for autonomous agents. NVIDIA's OpenShell/Sentry platform normalises the idea that an agent needs a sandbox with a hardware watchdog, while hindsight and paperclip keep racing to give agents more memory and more autonomy.
